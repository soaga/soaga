## 📊 Projects

### [Global Layoffs Analysis (2020–2023)](https://github.com/soaga/SQL-Project) — `SQL` `MySQL`
Cleaned a global layoffs dataset and used CTEs, window functions and structural aggregations to
rank companies by total layoffs and isolate repeat layoff events, then mapped the cyclical
pattern of workforce reductions across industries and countries.

### [Data Professionals Survey Dashboard](https://github.com/soaga/PowerBI-Project) — `Power BI` `DAX`
Normalised raw survey responses in Power Query, built a DAX model to compare salary, job
satisfaction and tool preference across roles and experience levels, and delivered an interactive
dashboard with KPI cards and cross-filtering.

### [Airbnb USA Market Analysis](https://github.com/soaga/Tableau-Project) — `Tableau`
Built a geographic dashboard of US Airbnb listings comparing pricing and revenue potential by
city and room type, quantifying which markets generate the highest revenue per listing.

### [Bike Sales Segmentation Study](https://github.com/soaga/Excel-Project) — `Excel`
Cleaned raw sales data and built a PivotTable dashboard with slicers to map income, age and
commute distance against purchase behaviour.
